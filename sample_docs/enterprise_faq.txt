[Corporate Travel & Reimbursement Policy]
1. Daily Meal Allowance: Employees traveling on business to Tier-1 metropolitan areas (e.g., Shanghai, Beijing, New York, London) are entitled to a daily meal subsidy of up to $25 USD (or 180 RMB); other cities up to $15 USD (or 100 RMB). No itemized receipts are required, but corporate travel check-in logs must be validated.
2. Transportation Standards: High-speed rail second-class is preferred for domestic journeys under 5 hours. Economy-class flights are permitted for cross-continental travel or journeys exceeding 5 hours.
3. Expense Claim Deadline: All expense reimbursement claims must be submitted via the corporate OA enterprise portal within 14 calendar days post-trip. Overdue claims will be automatically archived by the financial audit system.

[IT Security & Infrastructure Regulations]
1. Visitor Wi-Fi Credentials: The corporate guest Wi-Fi SSID is 'Enterprise-Guest', with credentials rotating automatically every Monday at 09:00 AM UTC. Contact the front desk or submit an IT service desk request for active guest passcodes.
2. Screen Lock Policy: Workstations must be locked immediately (Win + L or Cmd + Ctrl + Q) whenever left unattended for more than 3 minutes. Repeated violations will trigger an automated IT security review.
3. Production Database Access: Direct connections to production databases are strictly prohibited. Access must be routed exclusively through the Bastion JumpServer with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and audited session recording.

[Paid Time Off & Employee Benefits]
1. Annual Leave (PTO): Full-time employees with 1 full year of tenure receive 5 paid annual leave days; 3+ years receive 10 days; 5+ years receive 15 days. Unused PTO can be converted to salary compensation or rolled over to Q1 of the following fiscal year.
2. Birthday Privilege: Employees receive 1 paid birthday leave day in their birth month, along with a $50 gift card and anniversary certificate.
3. Health & Wellness Subsidy: Full-time employees are eligible for an annual gym membership reimbursement of up to $400 USD upon presentation of membership invoices.
